1Delete your account in the app (fastest)

Every account can be deleted from inside the Attendance Desk app:

  • Open the app and sign in.
  • Go to the Settings tab and scroll down to Danger Zone.
  • Principals tap Delete School Account. Teachers and students tap Delete My Account.
  • Tap Send Verification Code to My Email, enter the 6-digit code from your email, and confirm.
Deletion happens straight away and you are signed out. It cannot be undone.

2Can't open the app? Ask us by email

If you can't sign in (for example, your account is still waiting for approval, or you no longer have the app), email attendancestudentsapp@gmail.com from the email address you registered with, with the subject "Account deletion — [your school name]". Please include your name, your role (principal, teacher or student) and your school code if you know it.

  • We reply within 24 hours on business days, and may ask you to confirm the request from that email address.
  • Your account is deleted within 30 days, and we email you when it is done.

3What is deleted

AccountDeleted
Teacher or student Your login, your profile (name, email, school and class link, roll number for students), your record of accepting the Terms, and (for teachers) your assignment to classes.
Principal (whole school) The principal's login and profile, every teacher and student account of the school, all classes and subjects, the class lists and all attendance records, and the school's Terms acceptance records. An auto-renewing Monthly Pro subscription is cancelled first, so you are not charged again.

4What is kept, and for how long

  • Records that belong to the school. When a teacher or student deletes their own account, the school's class list and attendance records are not deleted, because the school keeps them. They are deleted when the principal deletes the school account, or within 90 days after the school account is closed. A student or parent can ask the principal to remove them sooner.
  • Payment and invoice records (amount, date, payment reference — never card or UPI details) are kept for 7 years, as Indian tax and accounting law requires.
  • A note that a school account was deleted (school name, principal's name and email, date) is kept with those payment records, so billing questions can still be answered.
  • Complaints sent to our Grievance Officer are kept as long as the law requires.
More detail is in our Privacy Policy (sections 8 and 9). Questions: attendancestudentsapp@gmail.com.
